# DeepSeek's 166K-Star Harness, Baidu's One-Shot OCR, and a 27B Model That Matches the Giants

The day in open source: DeepSeek's plugin-everything harness crossed 166K stars, Baidu shipped an OCR that parses whole documents in one pass, and a 27B model matched systems 60x its size. Plus: Modular freed Mojo under Apache 2, an AirTag caught Amazon scanning rare books, and Accenture's leaked audio confirmed the tokenpocalypse.

## AI & ML News

**Mojo Is Finally Open Source — Modular Ships the Compiler Under Apache 2**

Modular released the Mojo compiler and toolchain under an Apache 2 license, weeks after shipping Mojo 1.0. The language was announced in May 2023 as a Python superset; by August 2025 that promise was officially dead — "It's okay if it doesn't" remain a superset, the team said. The compiler now targets one job: making GPU programming as painless as possible with Python-inspired syntax. Apache 2 keeps it commercially usable with no copyleft strings, the same license that already powers most of the modern AI stack. [Source](https://www.modular.com/blog/mojo-open-source)

**A 27B Model Just Matched GPT-5.6 Luna — Qwen 3.8 Punches 60x Above Its Weight**

Qwen 3.8 27B scored 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — exactly matching GPT-5.6 Luna (max) and sitting one point behind GLM-5.2 (max, 753B params) and DeepSeek V4 Pro (max, 1.7T). Those rivals are 30-60x larger. A 27B model at that level, under Apache 2, changes the laptop inference math: frontier-adjacent capability without the frontier bill. [Source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen3-8-27b)

**An AirTag Just Caught an Amazon Warehouse Scanning Rare Books for AI Training**

404 Media tracked a suspicious bulk order of ~1,000 rare books with an AirTag and watched it land at Amazon's LAS8 facility in Las Vegas, where the VGT3 corner runs destructive book scanning. Amazon workers confirmed the practice in forum posts. It is the most direct evidence yet that AI-training data sourcing is running at industrial scale, far outside public view — and that the "licensed data" debate is not just about the big AI labs. [Source](https://www.404media.co/we-tracked-a-shipment-of-rare-books-it-ended-at-an-amazon-ai-training-facility/)

**Accenture's Leaked Audio Confirms the "Tokenpocalypse" — Non-Engineers Are Burning the Bill**

Leaked Accenture meeting audio, reported by 404 Media, shows the firm's own data: non-engineers now drive token consumption, and PDF-to-markdown conversion is one of the biggest token chewers. When a colleague joked "I hope you didn't just convert a PDF into images and then into markdown," the agentic AI strategy lead's answer was "that's what our data shows." Companies are scrambling to cap AI spend — the force behind cheaper models, aggressive caching, and the push to local inference. [Source](https://www.404media.co/the-tokenpocalypse-is-here-companies-are-scrambling-to-stop-spending-so-much-on-ai/)

## Big GitHub Releases

**DeepSeek Harness ⭐166K — "Everything Is a Plugin" Becomes the Week's Biggest Open-Source Release**

MIT-licensed, 12,404 commits, 17.6K forks. `npx @deepseek-ai/dsh web` boots a Web UI at :3080, and the harness itself is a plugin — there is now a whole `dsh-plugin` ecosystem topic and a Discord community around it. DeepSeek's answer to agent tooling sprawl is the largest repo on this list by an order of magnitude, and the star count says the community agrees. [GitHub](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness)

**Baidu's Unlimited-OCR ⭐24.1K — "Welcome the Era of One-shot Long-horizon Parsing"**

Baidu open-sourced an OCR system that parses entire documents in one pass instead of page-by-page — "unlimited OCR works" — a step-change for document pipelines, and precisely the kind of local preprocessing the tokenpocalypse discussion says enterprises should be running before agents touch files. Created within the last two months and already at 24K stars. [GitHub](https://github.com/baidu/Unlimited-OCR)

**grok-build ⭐25.2K — SpaceXAI's Rust TUI Enters the Coding-Agent Wars**

Apache-2.0, Rust core, fullscreen and mouse-interactive. grok-build ports codex and opencode tooling into its own harness, positioning it as the terminal-native challenger to Claude Code and Codex in the same developer workflow. The coding-agent wars are now being fought with TUI-first design. [GitHub](https://github.com/xai-org/grok-build)

**firecrawl/anydoc ⭐16.7K — Single-Digit-Millisecond Document Conversion, the Anti-Tokenpocalypse**

Word, PowerPoint, Excel, ODT, RTF, EPUB, CSV, and PDF to clean GitHub-Flavored Markdown in single-digit milliseconds. Rust core with Node, Python, and WASM bindings; one shared document model means a table-escaping fix for docx automatically fixes rtf and odt. It is the tool the Accenture leak says enterprises need: convert documents locally so agents never burn tokens re-reading raw formats. [GitHub](https://github.com/firecrawl/anydoc)

## Under-the-Radar Finds

**andrewyng/openworker ⭐14.8K — Andrew Ng's Self-Updating Agent Desktop**

OpenWorker (openworker.com) is in open beta: "fully usable, updates itself, and we're actively polishing rough edges." MIT-licensed, 2K forks, and it grew out of the aisuite project before moving to its own home. A self-updating desktop agent app from the man behind DeepLearning.AI — the "install once, never update manually" pattern applied to agent software. [GitHub](https://github.com/andrewyng/openworker)

The first mainstream-adjacent software category being rebuilt agent-first — with strong opinions about agents, in writing. [GitHub](https://github.com/trycompai/crm)

**awesome-dsh-plugin ⭐10K — The Plugin-Everything Era Got Its Own Awesome List Within Days**
