# DeepSeek's 167K-star harness, Mojo goes open source, and the tokenpocalypse hits enterprise AI

The day's most important technology news plus the GitHub repos that matter: DeepSeek's plugin-everything harness exploded past 167K stars, Mojo finally went open source, a 27B model matched the giants, and leaked Accenture audio revealed the tokenpocalypse inside enterprises.

## AI/ML News

**Mojo is now open source.** Modular released the Mojo compiler and toolchain under an Apache 2 license, weeks after shipping Mojo 1.0. The language was promised as a Python superset in May 2023, then officially pivoted away from that goal around August 2025: "It's okay if it doesn't" remain a superset, the team said. The compiler's job now is making GPU programming as painless as possible with Python-inspired syntax. Apache 2 means commercial use with no copyleft strings attached - the same license that powers most of the modern AI stack. [Source: Modular blog](https://www.modular.com/blog/mojo-open-source)

**Qwen 3.8 27B scores 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.** That equals GPT-5.6 Luna (max) and sits one point behind GLM-5.2 (max) and DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (max). The catch: those rivals are 753B and 1.7T parameters respectively. A 27B model matching that bar matters for anyone who wants capable AI on a laptop or a modest server, and Qwen keeps shipping these under Apache 2. [Source: Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen3-8-27b)

**An AirTag caught an Amazon facility scanning rare books.** 404 Media tracked a ~1,000-book order from a suspicious bulk buyer and found it delivered to Amazon's LAS8 facility in Las Vegas, where the VGT3 corner runs destructive book scanning. Amazon workers confirmed the practice in forum posts. It is the latest evidence of AI-training data sourcing running far outside public view. [Source: 404 Media](https://www.404media.co/we-tracked-a-shipment-of-rare-books-it-ended-at-an-amazon-ai-training-facility/)

**The tokenpocalypse is here, according to Accenture.** 404 Media reported on leaked Accenture meeting audio where the firm's own internal data shows non-engineers now drive token consumption. An agentic AI strategy lead named PDF-to-markdown conversion as one of the biggest token chewers. Companies are visibly scrambling to control AI spend, which explains the industry-wide push toward cheaper models, caching, and local inference. [Source: 404 Media](https://www.404media.co/the-tokenpocalypse-is-here-companies-are-scrambling-to-stop-spending-so-much-on-ai/)

## Big Famous GitHub Releases

**deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness - 167K stars.** "DeepSeek Harness: Everything is a Plugin." The largest repo on this list by a wide margin, and it is new. The plugin-everything architecture is DeepSeek's answer to agent tooling sprawl: every capability, including the harness itself, is a plugin. At 167K stars this is the biggest open-source release of the week, full stop. [GitHub](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness)

**xai-org/grok-build - 25.7K stars.** SpaceXAI's coding agent harness and TUI: fullscreen, mouse-interactive, extensible. The "build" naming plus a TUI-first design positions it as the terminal-native entry in the coding-agent wars, competing with Claude Code and Codex for the same developer workflow. [GitHub](https://github.com/xai-org/grok-build)

**firecrawl/anydoc - 17.2K stars.** Convert Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, RTF, EPUB, CSV, and PDF to clean Markdown. This is the anti-tokenpocalypse tool: it pre-processes documents locally into clean markdown so agents never have to burn tokens re-reading raw formats. Exactly the optimization the Accenture leak says enterprises need. [GitHub](https://github.com/firecrawl/anydoc)

## Under-the-Radar Finds

**guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover - 15.2K stars.** Strip multi-vendor AI provenance marks: Unicode text hygiene, statistical rewrite hooks. A tool that removes AI watermarks from text is controversial by design, but its star velocity says this is a conversation developers are having right now - and provenance-marking adoption will shape how tools like this evolve. [GitHub](https://github.com/guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover)

**eric-sabe/honey - 40 stars, rising.** A hands-off supply-chain watchdog for dev machines: orchestrates multiple security scanners (the new supply-chain attacks in open-source are growing). Tiny star count, but the right idea - automated dependency vetting that runs in the background. One to watch. [GitHub](https://github.com/eric-sabe/honey)

**Why it matters.** Two threads dominate: the economics of AI are forcing efficiency (cheaper models, local inference, document pre-processing like anydoc), and the platform race is being fought in open source - DeepSeek's 167K-star harness and SpaceXAI's grok-build are the new battlegrounds. Expect agent-harness consolidation to be the story of the next quarter.
